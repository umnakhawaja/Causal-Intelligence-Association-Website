About Us

Rigorous thinking,
open discussion

The Causality Intelligence Association is an interdisciplinary reading group at Oregon State. We meet weekly to work through foundational and cutting-edge papers on causal reasoning, machine learning theory, and the science of cause and effect.

Open to students, faculty, and researchers of all backgrounds — if you think carefully about how things work, you belong here.

Seminal Papers

Weekly Discussion

Each session is a genuine dialogue — questions encouraged, assumptions challenged, ideas tested.

Interdisciplinary

Members from AI, CS, Statistics, Philosophy, EECS, and beyond — causal thinking spans them all.
